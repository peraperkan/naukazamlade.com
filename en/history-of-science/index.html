Why are symmetry and energy conservation connected?

Release a pendulum and its speed changes, yet energy does not disappear without a trace. Repeat the experiment tomorrow and we expect the same rules of motion. There is a deep connection between these observations: the fact that physical laws do not change when we shift an experiment in time is linked to the conservation of energy. In 1918, mathematician Emmy Noether showed how such connections can be proved. Her idea helps us understand familiar experiments while also making us more careful when we ask about the energy of the entire universe.
