Educational games

Explore physics through play. Start the games here or on their creators’ websites.

Physics

Launchball

Place springs, slopes, magnets and other pieces to guide the ball to its goal. Predict what will happen, run the level, then change your design if it does not work. The puzzles let you explore forces, energy and problem solving.

The game is in English.

Physics

Crack the Circuit

Work out how the electrical circuit inside a locked box is connected. Your drawing must behave like the hidden circuit — simply lighting a bulb is not enough.

How to play

  1. Choose “New Game” and look carefully at which bulbs are lit.
  2. If there are switches, turn them on and off. Watch what changes and think about why.
  3. Build your own circuit on the board with a battery, bulbs, switches and wires. Connect them so it behaves like the hidden circuit.
  4. Check your solution. If the box stays locked, change the connections and try again. Later levels introduce series and parallel circuits and short circuits.
The game is in English and has 18 levels.

Physics

Magnetism Mini Golf

Guide the charged ball into the flagged hole. Place magnetic fields on the squares of the course to bend the moving ball’s path. Watch its trajectory, predict the turn and revise your layout if you miss.

How to play

  1. Start with the tutorial. Tap the ball or the club icon to take a shot, then watch where the ball goes.
  2. Drag a magnetic-field tile from the right onto a square of the course. “In” (×) points into the screen; “out” (•) points out of the screen.
  3. Check the ball’s charge sign (+ or −). The field direction and charge sign affect which way the moving ball bends.
  4. Take another shot and watch its path. If you miss the hole, use the circular arrow to retry; move the fields, and remove unneeded ones with the trash icon.
  5. Once the ball reaches the hole, continue to the next challenge. Try another field layout to understand why the path works.
The game is in English and begins with a short tutorial.
