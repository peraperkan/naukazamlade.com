Interactive chemistry

The interactive activities are in English.

01

Atomic mass and isotopes

Enter isotope masses and abundances. The calculator finds the weighted average atomic mass.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
02

Balancing chemical equations

Change the coefficients until each element has the same atom count on both sides. The live tally identifies any imbalance.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
03

Bond polarity

Compare two atoms’ electronegativities. See how their difference affects electron sharing in a bond.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
04

Electron configuration

Choose an element and follow how electrons fill orbitals. Compare the configuration with its shell diagram.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
05

Bohr model

Choose an atomic number from 1 to 118 and inspect the electron shells. Notice the outer-shell electron count.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
06

Interactive periodic table

Select an element to inspect its group, block and electron configuration. Change the color scheme to spot patterns.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
07

Bonding types

Pick two elements and compare electronegativities. The result suggests a bond type, though real bonds do not follow a single rigid threshold.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
08

Lewis structures

Choose a molecule and arrange bonding and lone electron pairs. Count valence electrons and check octet-rule exceptions.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
09

Stoichiometry step by step

Choose a reaction and read mole ratios from its coefficients. Convert reactant amounts into predicted product amounts.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
10

Molecular geometry

Select a molecule or a count of bonds and lone pairs. Compare its shape and angles using the VSEPR model.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
11

Molecule builder

Add atoms and connect them into molecules. See how composition and bonding determine the resulting structures.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
12

Noble gases

Compare group 18 elements, their properties and shells. Select a gas and inspect the importance of its outer shell.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
13

Periodic table game

Pick a set of elements and click the one requested. Missed elements return so you can practice them again.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
14

Periodic trends

Switch among electronegativity, ionization energy and electron affinity. Look for patterns across periods and down groups.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
15

States of matter

Add heat to ice and follow the temperature through melting and boiling. Flat parts of the heating curve mark phase changes.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
16

Gas laws

Change pressure, volume, temperature or amount of gas and examine the equations. Graphs help compare the different laws.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
17

The mole

Enter a formula and a mass or substance amount. Follow the conversion steps between grams, moles and particles.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
18

Particle box

Heat, cool or compress particles in a box. Watch how motion and collisions affect pressure and phase.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
19

pH scale

Select a value to compare acids, neutral solutions and bases. The calculator links pH with hydrogen-ion concentration.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
20

Reaction types

Choose a reaction pattern and predict products. Compare synthesis, decomposition, replacement and combustion.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
21

Valence electrons

Select a main-group element and inspect its Lewis symbol. Connect its table position with its valence-electron count.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
