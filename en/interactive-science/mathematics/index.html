Interactive mathematics

The interactive activities are in English.

01

Coordinate plane

Place points by dragging or entering coordinates. Examine their quadrants, distance and midpoint.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
02

Multiplication chart

Move through the chart to highlight a product. A rectangle shows how multiplication relates to area.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
03

Unit circle

Move the point or enter an angle in degrees or radians. Track sine, cosine and tangent, then try the recall exercise.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
04

Parabola explorer

Adjust a, b and c in a quadratic function. Watch the vertex, symmetry axis, intercepts and roots change.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
05

Point–slope form

Move the anchor point and set the slope. Compare point–slope form with y = mx + b.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
06

Slope of a line

Move points A and B on the grid. Follow rise over run, including horizontal and vertical lines.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
07

Slope–intercept form

Set the sliders for slope m and intercept b. See how each parameter moves or tilts the line.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
08

Systems of equations

Adjust two lines and locate their intersection. Try parallel or identical lines to see other solution cases.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
09

Vertex form of a parabola

Drag the vertex and change a. Connect the graph to y = a(x − h)² + k and inspect h and k.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
