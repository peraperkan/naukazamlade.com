Interactive physics

The interactive activities are in English.

01

Momentum and collisions

Set two carts’ masses, speeds and elasticity, then collide them. Compare total momentum and kinetic energy before and after.

Static and kinetic friction

Pull the block with increasing force. The graph shows static friction up to motion, then kinetic friction.

03

Free fall

Drop a hammer and feather on different worlds. Toggle air resistance and compare their fall times.

04

Newton’s second law: F = ma

Enter any two of force, mass and acceleration. Follow the calculation of the third and check the units.

05

Motion graphs

Move points on a position or velocity graph and watch the runner. Relate slope and area under the graph to motion.

06

Newton’s laws of motion

Explore rest, acceleration and interactions in three modes. Change force and mass to see their effects.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
07

Ohm’s law

Enter two of voltage, current and resistance. Observe the third value and power in the circuit.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
08

Projectile motion

Set launch speed and angle, then fire. Inspect trajectory, range, peak height and flight time.

Activity: PrepOK ↗Open activity in a new tab ↗
09

Simple machines

Choose a lever, ramp, pulley or wheel. Change its dimensions to compare force and distance.

10

Wave explorer

Adjust amplitude, wavelength and frequency. Switch between transverse and longitudinal waves and observe speed.

11

Net force

Electromagnetic spectrum

Move from radio waves to gamma rays. Compare wavelength, frequency and photon energy.
