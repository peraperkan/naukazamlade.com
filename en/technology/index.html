Headphones that pick one voice out of a noisy room

In a café, voices overlap with clattering dishes and music. Someone across the table starts speaking, but you catch only a few words. Our brains can often focus on a familiar voice, yet this is difficult in a crowded room. Researchers at the University of Washington presented a headphone prototype in 2024 that lets a user choose a speaker: turn towards that person, press a button, and hear their speech more clearly even as both people move.
